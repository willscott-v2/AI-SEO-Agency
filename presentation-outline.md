### Slide 23: LLM vs RAG vs AIO Diagram

### Slide 30: Schema Snippet Example
**Title:** Making Entities Machine-Readable

**Example:** JSON-LD schema for a local business

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "LocalBusiness",
  "name": "Search Influence",
  "description": "Digital marketing agency specializing in AI SEO",
  "url": "https://www.searchinfluence.com",
  "address": {
    "@type": "PostalAddress",
    "addressLocality": "New Orleans",
    "addressRegion": "LA"
  },
  "founder": {
    "@type": "Person",
    "name": "Will Scott"
  }
}
```

**Speaker Notes:**
This is schema markup - structured data that tells Google and AI exactly what you are. It's machine-readable. AI can parse this instantly and understand: Search Influence is a business, located in New Orleans, founded by Will Scott. Without schema, AI has to guess. With schema, AI knows. Implement clean schema on every important page.

### Slide 53A: AI SEO Mistakes I'm Seeing
**Title:** Don't Make These Mistakes

**Top 5 Mistakes:**

**1. Building on Broken Foundations**
- Jumping to AI SEO with slow sites, poor mobile UX, messy schema
- **Reality:** AI amplifies what you have - fix basics first

**2. Tool Obsession Without Strategy**
- Chasing every new AI tool without clear goals
- **Reality:** Strategy > tools every time

**3. Abandoning SEO Fundamentals**
- Thinking "AI SEO" replaces traditional SEO
- **Reality:** AI SEO builds on fundamentals, doesn't replace them

**4. Not Leading Client Conversations**
- Waiting for clients to ask about AI instead of proactively educating
- **Reality:** Clients need you to lead through this transition

**5. Analysis Paralysis**
- Waiting for the landscape to "settle" before acting
- **Reality:** First movers win; waiting means falling behind

**Speaker Notes:**
I'm seeing agencies make these five mistakes repeatedly. The biggest one - number one - is trying to do AI SEO on top of broken foundations. Clients come to me saying "We want AI optimization" but their site loads in 8 seconds, their mobile experience is terrible, their schema is a mess, their content is thin. AI can't fix that. It amplifies it. If your site is slow, AI crawlers time out. If your schema is wrong, AI cites you incorrectly. If your content is weak, AI ignores you or hallucinates. Fix the foundation first - speed, structure, schema, quality content - THEN layer on AI optimization.

Second mistake: tool obsession. Agencies are chasing every new AI tool without a clear strategy. Remember: tools serve strategy, not the other way around. Define what you're trying to achieve, then pick tools that support that goal.

Third: some agencies think AI SEO replaces traditional SEO. It doesn't. It builds on it. Links still matter. Content quality still matters. E-E-A-T still matters. AI SEO is an evolution, not a replacement.

Fourth: waiting for clients to ask about AI instead of leading the conversation. Your clients are seeing traffic declines and don't understand why. YOU need to be the one explaining it and providing the solution. Lead, don't follow.

Fifth: analysis paralysis. I hear "we're waiting to see how this plays out." The landscape won't settle for years. First movers are winning right now. Don't wait.
